The building

About Edge House Miami, by the numbers.

Edge House Miami is a 57-story tower at 1837 NE 4th Avenue in Edgewater, between Downtown Miami and the Miami Design District. Its residences, from studios to three bedrooms, sit on levels 11 to 56, each with a private terrace, and the building is approved for short-term rentals.

Every residence is delivered fully finished and furnished, curated by Adriana Hoyos, with paneled kitchens with Italian cabinetry, Bosch appliances and quartz countertops, 9-foot ceilings and floor-to-ceiling windows. Select two- and three-bedroom residences have a lock-out layout: two independent living spaces inside one home.

The developer is Grupo T&C, with architecture by Kobi Karp and landscape by Witkin Hults & Partners. The building runs like a hotel, with a welcome reception, concierge, a rental reservation system and guest check-in, and owners can join Edge Flex, an optional rental management program powered by WHOST that works with more than 85 booking platforms.

Location

Edgewater, on the bay.

1837 NE 4th Ave, Miami, FL 33132

Edgewater sits between Downtown Miami and the Miami Design District, steps from Biscayne Bay, with dining, shopping, parks and entertainment at the door. A waterfront pathway along the bay connects the neighborhoods for walking, jogging and cycling, and the Signature Bridge and The Underdeck, a 33-acre park beneath it, are coming soon.

Brightline, the free Metromover and the Miami Trolley link Edgewater to Downtown and Brickell. Dining close by includes Casadonna, Contessa, Michael's Genuine, Uchi, Kyu and L'Atelier de Joël Robuchon, and the Adrienne Arsht Center and Kaseya Center are a short drive away.

FAQ

Edge House Miami FAQ.

Ask on WhatsApp

How much does a residence cost?

Residences start from $574,000 for a studio in the current release. Prices change with each release, floor and plan; every available residence and its price is listed on this page.

Are the listed prices final?

Prices and availability change weekly with each release, floor and line. Your advisor confirms the price of any residence before you reserve it.

What is the deposit structure?

20% at contract (due within 48 hours of receipt of the offer), 10% 60 days after contract, 10% at the 25th-floor pour, 10% at top-off, and the 50% balance at closing.

Are the residences furnished?

Yes. Every residence is delivered fully finished and furnished, curated by Adriana Hoyos.

Can I rent my residence short-term?

Yes. Edge House Miami is approved for short-term rentals. Owners can manage rentals themselves or join Edge Flex, the building's optional rental management program powered by WHOST.

What is a lock-out residence?

Select two- and three-bedroom residences can work as two independent living spaces inside one home, with a discreet internal connection, so you can host, work or rent one side while you use the other.

What sizes are available?

Studios of 406 to 500 SF, one bedrooms of 554 to 627 SF, two bedrooms of 848 to 924 SF and a three-bedroom of about 1,232 SF, each with a private terrace.

Can international buyers purchase?

Yes, international buyers regularly purchase Miami pre-construction. We guide you through contracts, deposits by wire, and closing, in English, Spanish or Portuguese.

Does Edge House Miami offer EB-5?

Yes. The developer has an EB-5 offering of USD $1,050,000 per investor (standard, non-TEA): a loan-based investment in which 100% of the capital goes into the project, with 4% yearly interest under the loan agreement. It is offered only through the offering documents, to accredited investors. Review it with independent legal and immigration counsel.

When will it be completed?

Construction is underway, but the developer's current materials do not publish a delivery date. Ask us for the latest construction timeline; the deposit schedule is tied to the 25th-floor pour and top-off.

What are the monthly association dues?

Estimated dues are set out in the developer's condominium documents. We will send the current estimate with your price list.

How tall is the building?

Edge House Miami is a 57-story tower. Residences are on levels 11 to 56.

Who is developing Edge House Miami?

Grupo T&C is the developer, with architecture by Kobi Karp, interiors by Adriana Hoyos Design Studio and landscape by Witkin Hults & Partners. The condominium is being developed by Meridian TC Real Estate Group, LLC.

Does it cost more to buy through an advisor?

No. The price is the same as buying direct, and the developer pays the brokerage commission.
